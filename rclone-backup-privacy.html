rclone-backup privacy policy

rclone-backup is the OAuth client that the rclone program uses to copy your files to your Google Drive.

What rclone-backup can access

Through the drive.file scope, rclone-backup reaches only the Drive files that rclone created or opened with rclone-backup.

What we collect

We collect nothing, and rclone-backup has no server. rclone runs on your computers and keeps its Google sign-in token there.

Who receives data

Only Google receives data. Google stores the copied files in your Drive.

How long your data stays

The copied files stay in your Drive until you delete them. The sign-in token stays on your computers until you delete it or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This policy took effect on 2026-11-13.
